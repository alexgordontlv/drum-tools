E-drums: connect the Alesis module by USB and allow MIDI when Chrome asks. Hits are scored against the chart while the song plays.

Chart settings

Chart length. Bars added at the end copy the last bar.

Aligning a video: play it, pause exactly on the first drum hit of bar 1, press "Set to now". If the chart drifts later in the song, the BPM is slightly off: nudge it by 0.1 and watch the playhead against the snare hits.

Edit mode

Turn on Edit, then click a cell in the chart. Each click cycles: off → hit → accent → ghost (snare) / open (hi-hat) → off. Shift-click a column to clear it. Use the pattern buttons to fill the current bar quickly.

Keyboard shortcuts in edit mode

Click cell = toggle. Shift+click = clear column. Arrow keys move by bar. Charts are saved to this browser with "Save to browser"; "Download JSON" gives you a file to keep or share.

Where charts come from

Library charts marked "verified groove" were checked against the song's notation; the groove is right, section lengths are still approximate. Charts marked "unverified" are my best guess at the pattern and may be wrong: check them against the video's notation before trusting them. MIDI drum files of most famous songs exist online; import one and it becomes an exact chart. Or build your own in edit mode while looping the section.

YouTube only plays when this page is opened through "Start Play Along.command" (it serves the folder at localhost and opens Chrome). Nothing is downloaded from YouTube; the video plays through YouTube's own player. Local audio files work either way.
